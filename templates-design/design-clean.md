# Column — Visual System (Perfil 1: Corporate & Clean)

## Visual Philosophy
Institutional and trustworthy design system built on a cool cloud canvas (`#f6f6f8`). Deep indigo navy (`#111a4a`) serves as the primary structural color, paired with clean white surfaces (`#ffffff`), subtle hairline borders (`#e3e4e8`), and signal orange (`#ec652b`) for high-attention CTAs.

## Colors & Surfaces
- **Canvas/Page Background:** `#f6f6f8` (Cloud Canvas)
- **Card Surface:** `#ffffff` (Pure White)
- **Primary Text & Headings:** `#111a4a` (Indigo Navy) or `#3b3e47` (Slate Ink)
- **Secondary/Body Text:** `#7c7f88` (Steel)
- **Borders & Dividers:** `#e3e4e8` (Silver Lining)
- **Primary CTA / High Accent:** `#111a4a` (Navy) or `#ec652b` (Signal Orange)

## Component Rules
- **Border Radius:** Fixed `8px` (`rounded-lg`) for buttons, cards, inputs, and containers.
- **Cards:** White surface (`#ffffff`), `border border-[#e3e4e8]`, `p-6`, subtle low-opacity shadow (`shadow-sm`).
- **Buttons:** 
  - Primary CTA: Indigo Navy (`bg-[#111a4a] text-white rounded-lg px-5 py-3`).
  - High-Signal Accent: Signal Orange (`bg-[#ec652b] text-white rounded-lg px-5 py-3`).
  - Secondary: Outlined (`border border-[#111a4a] text-[#111a4a] bg-transparent rounded-lg px-5 py-3`).
