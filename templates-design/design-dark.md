# Lamborghini — Visual System (Perfil 2: Dark / Urban / Lifestyle)

## Visual Philosophy
Automotive-inspired dark stage layout. High contrast with deep carbon neutrals (`#202020`, `#000000`), sharp 0px corners, bold uppercase display typography, and a single high-signal yellow accent (`#ffc000` Giallo Vivo) reserved for primary action elements.

## Colors & Surfaces
- **Dark Hero Canvas:** `#202020` (Carbony Black) or `#000000` (Pure Black)
- **Light Content Section Background:** `#ffffff` (Pearl White) or `#f5f5f5` (Marble Gray)
- **Headings & Body Ink:** `#ffffff` (on dark) / `#202020` or `#000000` (on light)
- **Hairline Borders:** `#494949` (Dark Borders) / `#969696` (Light Borders)
- **Single Focal Accent / CTA:** `#ffc000` (Giallo Vivo) with black uppercase text

## Component Rules
- **Border Radius:** STRICTLY `0px` (`rounded-none`) across all cards, buttons, containers, and images.
- **Buttons:** 
  - Primary CTA: Solid Giallo Vivo (`bg-[#ffc000] text-black font-bold uppercase rounded-none px-6 py-4`).
  - Secondary/Ghost: Transparent with white or black border (`border border-[#7d7d7d] text-white uppercase rounded-none px-6 py-4`).
- **Cards & Grids:** Hard-edged containers (`rounded-none`), separated by background section contrasts (`#202020` vs `#ffffff`) or hairline rules rather than drop shadows.
- **Typography:** Heavy use of UPPERCASE headlines with generous tracking (`tracking-wider`).
