# MindMarket — Visual System (Perfil 3: Warm & Friendly)

## Visual Philosophy
Warm storybook canvas on cream paper (`#f5f1e4`) instead of cold white. Friendly, sticker-soft UI with 50px rounded radii on cards and buttons. Typography-led hierarchy using Inter with tight letter-spacing and strong contrast against dark ink (`#2c2e2a`).

## Colors & Surfaces
- **Canvas/Page Background:** `#f5f1e4` (Cream Paper) — ALWAYS use this for page background.
- **Card Surfaces:** `#ffffff` (Pure White) — Elevated white cards sitting on cream canvas.
- **Primary Text:** `#2c2e2a` (Ink Black) — Soft near-black for headings and body.
- **Primary Accent:** `#8ed462` (Fresh Grass) — Navigation highlights, toggle buttons, subtle borders.
- **Action CTAs:** `#ff705d` (Coral) or `#2c2e2a` ghost buttons with circular icon accents.

## Component Rules
- **Navigation:** Floating pill navigation bar (`bg-white rounded-[50px] shadow-none`).
- **Cards:** White cards on cream background with `rounded-[50px]`, `p-6` and subtle `#e0dbce` border or no border.
- **Buttons:** Pill buttons (`rounded-[50px]`), comfortable padding (`px-6 py-3`).
- **Typography Scale:** Bold, tight display headlines (`tracking-tighter font-medium`).
- **No Heavy Shadows:** Rely on surface color contrast (`#ffffff` on `#f5f1e4`) instead of drop-shadows.
